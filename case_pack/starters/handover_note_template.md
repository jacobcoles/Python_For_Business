# Handover note template

Copy these headings into your own note (right-click your project folder in the Explorer, choose **New File**, and name
it `handover.md`). They are the headings you used for the January pack in D1-S08. Anyone picking up your work should
be able to rerun it and know what it does and does not prove.

Write it from your working record (`worklog.md`), not from memory: the "what I checked" column answers the hardest
heading here, and it is the one nobody can reconstruct two days later.

```text
Purpose and period:
Inputs (exact files, and where they came from):
How to run it (the script or notebook, the settings, and the output folder):
What the result is, and where it is saved:
What I checked, and how (at least one figure checked independently of the code):
What happens when an input is missing or wrong:
What I did not change (raw data preserved):
Known limits and open questions:
Who to ask about the data or the rules:
```
